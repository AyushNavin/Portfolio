04 — What I've built

Projects

Math

The Batman Function

A generalized framework for combining multiple functions into a single expression. Its building block, A₁(L, n₁, n₂, l), uses floor, signum and ceiling to switch a piece on between n₁ and n₂ and zero it everywhere else, so separate pieces can simply be added together. The paper walks through the construction step by step, demos it with sin(x) followed by log(x), then applies it to the Batman logo.

The raw Batman curves were sourced from the internet; the framework that unifies them is my contribution. The paper also explains why a true function can never draw the full logo — one y per x.

I posted it on Reddit before joining Scaler — someone replied within 24 hours with a more optimized version. Humbling, but a good reminder to keep publishing work early.
